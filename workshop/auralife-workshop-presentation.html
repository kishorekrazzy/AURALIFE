COLLEGE WORKSHOP PRESENTATION

AURALIFE

A connected hospital-care platform for a clearer patient journey and a more coordinated hospital operation.

Prepared from the AURALIFE application prototype

01
THE MAIN POINT

Problem statement

Patients experience a hospital visit as disconnected tasks: choosing a hospital, finding a doctor, booking, waiting without clarity, locating a room, repeating information, and getting urgent help. Staff must coordinate the same journey from separate operational views.

The gap creates uncertainty for patients and avoidable pressure at reception, in queues, and during urgent cases.

02
WHY THIS PROBLEM MATTERS

One fragmented visit creates several points of failure

Before arrival

Patients may not know which hospital, department, or doctor suits their need.

At the hospital

A token alone does not explain the queue, estimated wait, room, or accessible route.

During urgent care

Staff need context early enough to prepare, prioritize, and direct the patient safely.

Design question: How can one app give every role the right operational information at the right moment?

03
THE PROPOSED RESPONSE

A single digital front door for the hospital visit

Guide

Help people choose hospitals, departments, doctors, appointments, and routes.

Coordinate

Keep the patient, staff, and administrator aligned on tokens, queue state, capacity, and tasks.

Escalate

Send emergency alerts and ambulance requests into a visible priority workflow.

AURALIFE supports the operational journey around care. Clinical decisions remain with qualified health professionals.

05
ROLE-BASED EXPERIENCE

Shared operational context, tailored to each user

Patient or attendant

  • Hospital and doctor selection
  • Appointment, payment mock-up, token, and live queue
  • Maps, ambulance availability, records, and feedback

Staff workspace

  • Walk-in check-in and real token creation
  • Current queue and next-patient control
  • Tasks and patient-facing coordination

Admin dashboard

  • Queue, no-show, capacity, and satisfaction visibility
  • Emergency acknowledgment
  • Appointment, feedback, ambulance, and analytics oversight
06
IMPLEMENTATION SNAPSHOT

Prototype architecture

Vite client

Role-based UI, patient language choices, navigation, queue display, and service interactions.

→

Express API

Catalog, appointments, operations, and assistant routes make the workflows available to the client.

→

Seeded JSON state

Demo hospitals, 15 departments, doctors, destinations, ambulances, appointments, feedback, and tasks.

The server computes operational analytics from stored appointment and feedback records rather than displaying only fixed dashboard values.

08
VALUE AND RESPONSIBILITY

What the prototype demonstrates, and what production would require

Demonstrated value

  • A clearer patient journey across booking, waiting, navigation, and feedback
  • A visible emergency priority workflow across patient and admin views
  • Shared queue and operational information for multiple hospital roles
  • Multilingual patient-facing labels in English, Telugu, and Hindi

Production requirements

  • Authentication, role-based authorization, encryption, and audit review
  • Clinical governance and verified emergency escalation procedures
  • Integration with hospital scheduling, records, maps, and ambulance dispatch
  • Privacy, consent, accessibility, reliability, and health-data compliance testing
09
WORKSHOP TAKEAWAY

Better hospital care starts with better coordination

AURALIFE connects the moments around care so patients know what to do next and hospital teams can respond with the same operational context.

Core message: reduce uncertainty for patients and reduce coordination gaps for hospitals.
